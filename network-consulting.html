Network Consulting

Network Design & Infrastructure Consulting

Resilient, high-performance networks built for how your business actually operates

A network is the foundation everything else runs on — when it's under-designed, every application, call, and file transfer feels it. We design and review network infrastructure with a focus on resilience, security, and long-term maintainability, translating business requirements into a network that performs predictably as you grow.

What We Cover

End-to-end network engagements

Network Design & Architecture

Purpose-built topology, addressing, and routing design for offices, data centres, and hybrid cloud environments — scoped to your growth plans, not just today's headcount.

Segmentation & VLAN Design

Logical separation of traffic — users, servers, guest, IoT, and management — to contain risk, simplify policy, and keep east-west traffic under control.

Wireless Network Design

Site-surveyed, capacity-planned Wi-Fi design covering coverage, roaming, and RF interference — not just "put an access point in every room."

Redundancy & High Availability

Failover links, redundant paths, and resilient routing and switching so a single link or device failure doesn't take the business down with it.

Performance Optimisation & Troubleshooting

Root-cause analysis of latency, congestion, and intermittent faults, backed by packet captures and real traffic data — not guesswork.

Documentation & Handover

Clear network diagrams, IP schemas, and configuration documentation your internal team — or next provider — can actually use.

Our Approach

Scoped to your environment, not a template

Whether you're standing up a new site, untangling a network that's grown organically over several years, or need an independent second opinion before a major change, engagements start with understanding how the network actually gets used day to day.

Every recommendation is prioritised by real-world impact — the changes that reduce risk or improve reliability first, with a clear, staged path for the rest, so budget and downtime are always used where they matter most.
